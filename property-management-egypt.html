Operational FAQ

Can you manage my Cairo property if I live abroad?

Absolutely. Our infrastructure is expressly designed for overseas property owners, expats, and international investors. We act as your local operational partner in Egypt, handling guest communication, maintenance, and housekeeping transparently.

Do you outsource your turnover cleaning?

No. All turnover and deep cleaning services are executed exclusively by our dedicated in-house division, Elhabib Clean. This ensures strict quality control and consistent hygiene standards.

What areas of Cairo do you currently operate in?

We currently provide on-ground property management and turnover operations in Maadi, Katameya, Zahraa El Maadi, Nasr City, New Cairo, and Fifth Settlement (Tagamoa).
