Frequently Asked Questions

Can you manage my Cairo property if I live abroad?

Yes. Our services are specifically designed for overseas property owners, expats, and international investors. We act as your local operational partner, handling guest communication, maintenance, and housekeeping with high ethical standards.

Do you provide turnover cleaning services?

Yes. All turnover and deep cleaning services are executed by our dedicated in-house division, Elhabib Clean. This ensures strict quality control and consistent hygiene standards between guest stays, rather than relying on unverified third-party contractors.

What areas of Cairo do you currently serve?

We currently provide on-ground property management and turnover cleaning operations in Maadi, Katameya, Zahraa El Maadi, Nasr City, New Cairo, and Fifth Settlement (Tagamoa).

How do you handle property maintenance?

We conduct routine property inspections and coordinate emergency repairs. Minor issues are resolved quickly to ensure guest satisfaction, while major repairs are communicated to you for approval with transparent local cost reporting.
