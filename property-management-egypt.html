Operational FAQ

Can you manage my Cairo property if I live abroad?

Absolutely. Our infrastructure is expressly designed for overseas property owners, expats, and international investors. We act as your local operational partner, handling strict guest communication, maintenance, and housekeeping with high ethical standards.

Do you outsource your turnover cleaning?

No. All turnover and deep cleaning services are executed exclusively by our dedicated in-house division, Elhabib Clean. This ensures strict quality control, accountability, and consistent hygiene standards between guest stays, entirely bypassing the unreliability of third-party contractors.

What areas of Cairo do you currently operate in?

We currently provide on-ground property management and turnover operations in Maadi, Katameya, Zahraa El Maadi, Nasr City, New Cairo, and Fifth Settlement (Tagamoa).

How do you handle property maintenance and asset protection?

We conduct routine property inspections and immediately coordinate emergency repairs. Minor issues are resolved quickly to protect your reviews, while major repairs are communicated to you for approval alongside transparent local cost reporting, protecting you from inflated prices.
